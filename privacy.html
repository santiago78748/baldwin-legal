Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy covers the private bookkeeping backup tool operated by Baldwin Investment Group LLC ("we", "us"). The tool is for internal use by Baldwin Investment Group LLC only and is not offered to the public or to any other business.

What the tool accesses

With our authorization, the tool connects to the QuickBooks Online account of Baldwin Investment Group LLC through the Intuit QuickBooks API. It reads accounting data (transactions, accounts, customers, vendors, items, reports, and attachments) so that a backup copy can be saved.

How data is used and stored

Data is copied to computer storage controlled by Baldwin Investment Group LLC and is used only for backup, record keeping, and restoration of our own books. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer and are not shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, publish, or share QuickBooks data with any third party, other than service providers we choose to use for our own accounting, tax, or legal needs.

Retention and revocation

Backups are kept for as long as we need them for business and legal record keeping. Access can be revoked at any time from the Intuit account's connected apps settings.
